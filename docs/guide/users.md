# User guide

For vets, pharmacists, store staff and anyone who reads reports. Vetflow works on a phone, a tablet and a computer. What you see depends on your role.

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| **Vet** | Patients, consultations, prescriptions (write and approve), reports |
| **Pharmacist** | Dispense, issue stock, receive and count stock, transfers, reports |
| **Inventory manager** | Everything about stock and purchasing, counter sales, imports, adjustments, audit reports |
| **Admin** | Everything, plus users, branches and settings |
| **Viewer** | Read-only: patients, stock and reports |

If a button you expect is missing, your role does not allow it. Ask an admin.

## Signing in

Open the address your clinic gave you and sign in with your email and password. Five wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes. If you forget your password, ask your clinic admin to set a new one (email reset is not set up). Change the password you were given the first time you sign in.

On a phone you get a tab bar at the bottom. The round **Record** button is in the middle, and **More** holds the rest. On a tablet there is a narrow menu at the side, and on a computer a full menu.

**Today** is your starting page: stock value, low-stock items, batches expiring within 30 days and already expired, a short list of what needs attention (with a link to all alerts), the most used items, and recent stock movements.

## Vets: record a consultation

1. Open **Patients**, find the animal (search by animal name, owner or phone), or register a new patient. Then start a consultation. On a phone you can also tap **Record**.
2. **Record or type.** Tap the microphone and speak the consultation, or upload an audio file, or choose **Type or paste a dictation instead**. Recording needs your browser's permission to use the microphone, and a clinic that has speech turned on. If it is off or not set up, typing always works.
3. **Draft.** Vetflow turns the words into a draft: notes and prescription lines. Medicines are matched to your clinic's own list, including names the recogniser got wrong, such as a drug name split in two. Anything it could not match is marked for you to choose.
4. **Notes.** Read the draft and finish it yourself. Nothing is filled in for you as a final answer: you write or confirm the diagnosis and the plan. What was said is shown exactly as heard.
5. **Prescription.** Check each line: medicine, strength, dose, how often, how long, and quantity. Add, remove or change lines. A medicine that is not in the clinic's list cannot be dispensed from stock, so add it to the list or choose a similar one.
6. **Approve.** Only a vet can approve. You are asked to confirm, and from then on the prescription is locked. To change an approved prescription, **Cancel prescription** (a reason is required) and write a new one.

If you leave halfway, the consultation is kept as a draft. Find it under the patient and carry on.

A voice draft is a suggestion. Read it as you would read a note from a new colleague.

## Pharmacists: dispense

1. Open **Dispensing**. The list shows approved prescriptions with medicine still to give out.
2. Open one. The dialog shows which batches will be used, **earliest expiry first**, and the quantity from each. Expired batches are never offered.
3. For a **controlled medicine** a second person must witness. Choose the witness and they enter their own password on your screen. Both names are recorded.
4. Confirm. Only now does stock go down, and the movement is recorded with your name. Approving a prescription alone does not change stock.

A **counter sale** (an owner buying without a prescription) is for roles allowed to sell over the counter, and is recorded the same way.

## Store staff: stock

Open **Inventory**. Across the top: **Items, Receipts, Orders, Suppliers, Transfers, Counts, Open vials, Alerts, Import**. You see the ones your role allows.

- **Items.** Search by name or brand, or scan a barcode if your clinic has barcodes switched on. Open an item to see stock by batch (earliest expiry first), minimum levels, and **Movement history** (every change, who made it and when). Near-identical names get a warning when you add an item, to avoid duplicates.
- **Adjust stock.** For damage, expiry write-off, return to supplier, or a count correction. A reason is always required and is saved. There are no silent edits.
- **Receipts.** Record a delivery as a draft: supplier, invoice, batch number, expiry date, quantity and cost (in rupees). You can attach a photo of the invoice. **Stock does not change until you confirm the receipt.**
- **Orders.** Create a purchase order as a draft, send it (inventory managers and admins can send), and receive against it (pharmacists can receive). Partial deliveries are fine: the order shows what is still outstanding.
- **Transfers.** Move stock between stores. The sender sends it, the receiver confirms what arrived. Stock is "in transit" in between, and a shortfall is shown as a difference to resolve.
- **Counts.** Start a count for a store, enter what you find, and review the differences. Each difference needs a reason before it can be posted.
- **Open vials.** For multi-dose vials: record each draw, and see how long the vial can still be used.
- **Alerts.** Expired and soon-to-expire batches, and items at or below their minimum.
- **Import.** Upload an Excel file of items or opening stock. You see a preview with every problem marked, and nothing is saved until you confirm.

Money is shown in rupees and dates in India time.

## Reports

Open **Reports** and pick one of: **Current stock** (also as at an earlier date), **Valuation** (at the actual cost of each batch), **Movements**, **Expiry**, **Low stock**, **Purchases**, **Consumption**, **Wastage**, and **Audit log** (admins and inventory managers). Filter by dates and store, then download **CSV** or **Excel**, or print the page (the browser can save it as a PDF).

## Tips

- Everything important asks you to confirm first. Read the confirmation: it says what will happen and cannot be taken back by the same screen.
- If something looks wrong, do not work around it. Note the item or prescription, the time, and tell your admin; every action is in the audit log.
- Text is never smaller than 12 pixels and buttons are made for fingers, so a phone is fine for most work.
